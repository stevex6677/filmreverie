## Delivered behavior

- Versioned IndexedDB `darkroom-rolls` stores roll metadata, stable frame records, untouched original Blobs, normalized viewing JPEGs, and thumbnails. One read/write transaction commits a complete roll; failed and cancelled imports do not leave visible half-rolls. Missing asset records fail clearly. Version upgrades retain existing stores/data.
- Imports validate JPEG/PNG signatures and dimensions before decode, normalize EXIF once, naturally sort filenames, detect identical content with SHA-256, and require explicit retention/removal of duplicates. Failed files must be removed before Save enables. Drafts support drag and keyboard reordering, 90° rotation, cover choice, and cancellation.
- 35mm uses up to six exposures per strip. 120 uses up to four for 6×4.5, three for 6×6/6×7, two for 6×9. The last strip ends at its real frame count. 120 has wider stock, no perforations, no 35mm half-frame numbers, no DX codes, and no backing-paper exposure numbers. Its stock-name edge print is deliberately simplified.
- Every scan uses non-destructive fit. Aspect/count mismatches are explained rather than cropped, stretched, duplicated, or rejected solely for an incomplete roll. Rotation metadata applies to the viewing shader and cover/thumbnail presentation; source Blobs remain unchanged. M9 positive viewing applies no new stock-dependent photographic grade.
- The library supports open, rename, stock/format edits, order/rotation/cover edits, and Trash/Restore. There is no purge or automatic deletion. Removing the open roll returns to the library and the built-in example remains available.
- Switching prepares the new overview before replacing the old roll, rejects stale asynchronous results, and restores stable frame identity, inspection level/pose, allowed viewing mode, brightness and loupe magnification. Edits reset invalid framing while preserving valid frame identity. E100 stays positive-only. Modal dialogs own keyboard focus; the scene pauses rendering during import/review. Long frame maps scroll within the sidebar, including in short desktop windows. Explicit built-in/example/fixture choices override automatic saved-roll restoration; opening a saved roll clears obsolete dataset query flags.
- Only thumbnails are read for library covers. Opening a saved roll reads viewing derivatives, checks original membership without loading original bytes, and never decodes originals. Detail textures are bounded to three strips near selection; three loading workers prioritize overview and adjacent detail. Changing the detail window releases its owned texture cache. Roll replacement/disposal revokes its object URLs. Four repeated switches left exactly four runtime URLs for the active two-frame roll.

## Limits and storage scope

Import limits: 1–72 files, at most 40 MB and 40 megapixels per file, 300 MB per batch, one decode at a time. Viewing derivatives are bounded to a 2048-pixel long edge and thumbnails to 256 pixels. Transparent PNG backgrounds are composed onto black in the viewing derivative; originals are preserved. Browser memory and GPU limits still apply.

Saved rolls belong to the browser profile **and origin**. Keep `http://127.0.0.1:5193` when checking persistence. A different browser, hostname or port has a different library. Clearing site data removes the library. The app requests durable storage where supported and shows approximate site storage usage; denial does not promise a permanent backup. Quota and disabled-storage failures are explicit. No image bytes, names, or roll metadata are posted to a server. Keep originals separately; backup export/import is outside M12.

## Format references and deliberate approximations

Gate dimensions in millimeters are 36×24, 41.5×56, 56×56, 68.5×56 and 82.6×56. They represent particular common camera gates rather than treating nominal names as exact aspect ratios; other camera models vary. The 6×4.5 gate is oriented across the film's 56 mm usable width, so a landscape scan often needs a 90° rotation. The overall film width is modeled as 61 mm for 120 and 35 mm for 135.

- [Mamiya 645AF specifications](https://mamiya.co.uk/cameras/645afd/specs.html): 56×41.5 mm actual image size.
- [Hasselblad 501CM / 503CW manufacturer manual](https://cdn.hasselblad.com/manuals/501CM_%26_503CW_Manual/1.0.0.0/501CM--503CW.pdf): 120/A12 6×6 film system reference.
- [Fujifilm GW690 / GW670 manufacturer manual, archived scan](https://antiquecameras.net/images/fuu.pdf): actual gates 56×82.6 mm and 56×68.5 mm.
- [Kodak professional film catalog](https://www.kodakprofessional.com/sites/default/files/wysiwyg/film/2021_Pro_Film_Catalog_List.pdf) and [Portra 800 edge-print specification](https://kodakprofessional.com/photographers/film/color/kodak-professional-portra-800-film/528): 120 stock availability and manufacturer identity.

The 120 edge font, exact factory printing registration and emulsion batch codes are not reconstructed. The viewer prints stock identity only, preserves M9's physical base appearance, and leaves exposure identifiers in separate navigation. Strip cutting/grouping is a display choice. Source photographs are positive scans; negative preview remains the established display approximation.
